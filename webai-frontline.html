Overview

webAI Frontline brings knowledge retrieval to the field: offline, on-device Q&A on iPad, grounded in an organization’s own documents. Technicians and operators ask questions in context and get answers with citations — without relying on a network connection.

It is built for environments where manuals, schematics, and procedures are dense, updates are constant, and work happens far from a desk — aviation, manufacturing, oil and gas, defense, energy and utilities, and transportation.

The Problem

Knowledge trapped in documents

Field workers often need a precise answer from thousands of pages of procedures, maintenance manuals, and schematics — while standing next to equipment, sometimes in air-gapped or low-connectivity sites.

Manual search across engine changes, revisions, and complex document types slows the job. Cloud-only AI is a poor fit when data cannot leave the premises and citations must point back to the source of record.

Research

KLM & Air New Zealand

Research with KLM and Air New Zealand grounded the product in real line work: technicians, document management, and operations stakeholders who live inside maintenance and procedure systems every day.

That work shaped how Frontline scopes collections to a task, handles complex and schematic documents, and keeps answers cited to the page — so trust stays with the organization’s own materials.

My Contribution

Design & research for knowledge retrieval

As Design & Research Lead for Knowledge Retrieval, I led how Frontline surfaces answers from organizational knowledge on-device: Knowledge Graph RAG, cited source pages, and task-scoped collections that keep search bounded to what a technician needs for the job at hand.

The experience is built for complex and schematic documents, offline and air-gapped use on iPad, and a content path that can move from upload through deploy, refresh, and expand without breaking field trust.

Content Lifecycle

Upload → deploy → refresh → expand

Frontline treats documentation as a living system — not a one-time dump onto a device.

Upload

Bring organizational manuals, procedures, and schematics into task-scoped collections.

Deploy

Refresh

Expand

Grow coverage across fleets, sites, and document types without losing citation discipline.
