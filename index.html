MONA: Web3 3D Creator Platform and Marketplace

MONA is a blockchain-powered 3D creator platform and marketplace designed to empower artists, game developers, and digital creators to seamlessly build, customize, and monetize immersive virtual experiences. The product ecosystem includes a digital asset marketplace (NFTs), a multiplayer online 3D metaverse, and a Unity-powered 3D creation tool that enables users to easily design, play, and own their own virtual worlds. Through extensive product mapping and user research, we identified a need for a low-barrier, creator-friendly toolset that removes the complexity of traditional 3D development. This led to the development of MONA Create: a powerful, intuitive worldbuilding platform that allows creators to craft interactive multiplayer experiences without requiring advanced Unity or development expertise. Whether for virtual events, social gaming, or digital art exhibitions, MONA enables anyone to participate in the metaverse economy with ease.
