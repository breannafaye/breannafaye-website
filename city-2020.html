Overview

City 2020 was a strategic foresight project asking a core question: what is the smart city in 2020, and who is it for? The work looked at the values that would shape future cities and how Samsung could build toward them. It ended in five opportunity areas, persona-led scenarios and strategic recommendations for Samsung.

Approach

Research → Analysis → Hypothesis

1. Research

Mapped trends and projections across 10 verticals: smart home, automotive, manufacturing, education, work, money, healthcare, personal media & entertainment, retail and hospitality.

Ran ethnographic interviews in New York, Paris and Singapore, three cities projected to be future mega-cities.

Conducted expert interviews with 13 subject-matter experts across the ten verticals.

Studied four distinct generations to understand which user values would carry into 2020.

2. Analysis

Synthesized 1,000+ research cards from the research.

Modeled each vertical’s research against that industry’s core frameworks, which surfaced six key experiential themes.

Used the ethnographic data to build city-specific personas for New York, Paris and Singapore.

3. Hypothesis

Combined the themes and vertical trends into five high-level opportunity areas: business opportunities that cut across verticals and are united by shared emerging needs.

Developed each area with design tools including user scenarios, concept maps and ecosystem maps.
